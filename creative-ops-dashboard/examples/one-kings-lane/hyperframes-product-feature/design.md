# One Kings Lane Motion Adapter

This adapter consumes the portable One Kings Lane package. It does not redefine the canonical brand system.

## Concept angle

A five-second winter-garden product portrait that uses the fal.ai fountain clip as its live visual plate, then layers deterministic brand typography and calls to action over it like a shelter-magazine opener.

## Palette

- Heritage deep: `#06312B`
- Heritage green: `#3B5F58`
- Heritage bright: `#1E5F53`
- Powder: `#C6D0DC`
- White: `#FFFFFF`
- Editorial ink: `#323232`

## Typography

- Editorial display: Chronicle Display; use Georgia when the licensed file is unavailable.
- Interface and labels: Sofia Pro; use Arial when the licensed file is unavailable.
- Body: Lato; use Arial when the licensed file is unavailable.
- Display weight: 400. Interface labels: 700–800.

## Frame behavior

- Full-bleed fal.ai video plate with all identity, typography, and action layers kept outside the generated footage.
- Square-edged solid copy field for dependable contrast.
- Left-anchored hierarchy with image detail remaining visible on the right.
- Product geometry and material remain visually authoritative.
- Brand identity and copy are crisp controlled layers, never generated into footage.

## Motion language

- Measured, confident, observational.
- Entrances use fades, 8–24px rises, or quiet horizontal reveals.
- Stagger hierarchy by 100–180ms and overlap entrances.
- Use `expo.out` for the editorial title, `power2.out` for labels, and `sine.inOut` for image drift.
- Keep a readable hold of at least 1.2 seconds.
- Use blur crossfades, focus pulls, or editorial push slides between related scenes.
- Use one transition family per sequence; no bounce, elastic overshoot, glitch, strobe, whip pan, or distorted logos.

## Reduced motion

Remove image scale travel and parallax. Preserve the same final composition, hierarchy, timing order, and readable hold using opacity only.

## Do not

- Do not add radius to major fields.
- Do not use generic hotel luxury, neon light, glossy CGI, or kinetic effects that compete with the product.
- Do not redraw, deform, or animate the logo by shape.
- Do not ask generated footage to render type, price, offer, interface, or identity.
