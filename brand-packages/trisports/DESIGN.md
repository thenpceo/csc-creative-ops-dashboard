## Brand in one sentence

TriSports was a specialist endurance-commerce surface for athletes who shop across swim, bike, and run. Its final standalone design was dense and equipment-led, using magenta-red for action, endurance blue for secondary structure, and orange for energy. The live domain now redirects to Competitive Cyclist's triathlon collection; this file preserves the last first-party identity.

## Layout, spacing, shape, and depth

Organize the catalog around swim, bike, run, and complete triathlon systems. Use a compact grid and preserve cross-discipline comparison context; key fit and compatibility details belong close to price and stock.

Small 3–5px radii support controls and badges. The core catalog remains rectilinear and utilitarian.

Use borders and pale neutral layers. Menus, filters, and quick-view overlays may have a restrained shadow; product cards stay flat.

Spacing values: 4px, 8px, 12px, 16px, 24px, 32px, 48px. Preserve a 44px minimum interactive target and explicit focus treatment.

## Actions and graphic hierarchy

Primary actions use magenta-red; blue supports navigation and informational states. Orange is a controlled energy accent. Discipline tags should remain textual and understandable without color alone.

Use one primary message and one primary action per decision area or fixed asset. Recompose every aspect ratio. Current promotion, price, inventory, fitment, availability, dates, and service scope are removable runtime layers.

## Web and interactive guidance

Navigation and decision hierarchy must support **discipline, race distance, fit, compatibility, transition efficiency, training use, and performance**. Collection, product, guide, and service patterns live in modules/interactive.json; product hierarchy and current-data behavior live in modules/commerce.json.

Compact layouts preserve the same decision order. Overlays must not cover identity, current facts, fitment or options, form errors, or the primary action.

## Photography and image direction

- **Environment:** open water, pool, road cycling, running routes, transition areas, training, and race preparation.
- **Camera:** discipline-specific action, transition detail, aerodynamic product views, and honest training coverage.
- **Lighting:** clear early-day endurance light with controlled product and water reflections.
- **Palette and materials:** historical blue, orange, black, white, water, asphalt, and high-visibility race color.
- **People:** real endurance athletes across experience levels using correct position, equipment, and transition behavior.
- **Avoid:** presenting the identity as currently standalone, invented race claims, wrong aerodynamic fit, single-discipline generic fitness, unsafe open-water context.

Product and service truth remains locked across studio, context, detail, and human-use images. Real product media remains the factual anchor.

## Image prompt structure

1. Job intent and real use case.
2. Stable subject and authorized references.
3. open water, pool, road cycling, running routes, transition areas, training, and race preparation.
4. Composition and copy-safe region.
5. discipline-specific action, transition detail, aerodynamic product views, and honest training coverage.
6. clear early-day endurance light with controlled product and water reflections.
7. Material, geometry, scale, people, and safety locks.
8. Explicit exclusions and no generated words or logos.

Use modules/generative-image.json for environment-wide, product-studio, product-context, and people-in-use structures.

## Motion design

three-discipline rhythm, directional continuity, transition-match cuts, and legible technical overlays.

Motion design uses controlled typography, layout, verified product media, and dimensional layers. One movement idea belongs to each beat. Identity and essential copy are composited as crisp controlled layers. Provide reduced motion.

## Generative video

Generative video produces clean visual plates. Each shot uses one stable subject, one subtle action, one camera move on one axis, explicit duration and ratio, light and material direction, continuity locks, and exclusions. Composite identity, verified copy, captions, interface, prices, and offers afterward.

Reject morphing products, drifting features, warping environments, anatomy failures, fake text, invented identity, and incoherent materials.

## Spatial and dimensional expression

water behavior, aerodynamic geometry, running footwear, transition layouts, timing accents, and accurate athlete scale. Dimensional scenes preserve verified scale, product geometry, contact, material response, and readable silhouettes. Effects must explain function, environment, or material.

## Storyboards and sequential production

Preferred narrative rhythm:

1. State the race or training objective.
2. Connect the three disciplines.
3. Prove one equipment or transition advantage.
4. Show sustained effort.
5. Resolve with specialist guidance.

Every shot record states story function, duration, ratio, visual intent, composition, camera, action, lighting, exact approved copy, audio intent, continuity locks, asset selectors, rights, and evaluation references.

## Voice

Prefer specific language about discipline, race distance, fit, compatibility, transition efficiency, training use, and performance. Avoid generic prestige, invented expertise, false urgency, unsupported results, and engagement claims without current evidence.
